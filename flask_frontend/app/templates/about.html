About LocalHarvest

LocalHarvest connects customers with local farmers and organic food producers. Our mission is to make fresh, healthy food accessible to everyone while supporting small farms and reducing environmental impact.


Some of our sources include:

  • Local farms
  • Farmer's markets
  • Community supported agriculture programs
  • Organic food producers
